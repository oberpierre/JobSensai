Strategic Partnerships Development Manager, Android Ecosystem Partnerships

info_outline
XGoogle will be prioritizing applicants who have a current right to work in Singapore, and do not require Google's sponsorship of a visa.

Minimum qualifications:

  • Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience.
  • 7 years of experience in the mobile network operator/OEM industry.
  • Experience managing agreements and partnerships.

Preferred qualifications:

  • Experience working with C-level executives and cross-functionally across all levels of management.
  • Experience in business development or partner management, understanding long business cycles, negotiating agreement.
  • Experience working in Vietnam and Pakistan markets.
  • Track record of strategic capability within the mobile ecosystem, telecommunications, or tech-adjacent industries.
  • Ability to communicate in English and Vietnamese fluently to navigate and support local partnerships in South East Asia (SEA) markets.

About the job

Google's line of products and services to our clients never stops growing. The Partnerships Development team is responsible for seeking and exploring new opportunities with Google's partners. Equipped with your business acumen and extensive product knowledge, you are right on the front line of interacting with our partners, and helping them find ways to grow using Google's newest product offerings. Your knowledge of relevant verticals and relationships with key industry players will help shape our great applications and content for products such as YouTube, Google TV and Commerce.

As a Strategic Partnerships Development Manager, you will be at the forefront of driving growth for Android and platform and devices product areas across the telco, Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) and retail sector. You will be responsible for designing partnership strategies and executing complex agreements that are critical to drive our user acquisition and business generation efforts in the region with partners. As a member of the APAC Android Business Development team, you will work closely with Android Product, Engineering, Marketing, and Legal teams on new initiatives and key strategic relationships. In this role, you will identify industry trends and key influencers, managing the process from start to finish.Android is Google’s mobile operating system powering more than 3 billion devices worldwide. Android is about bringing computing to everyone in the world. We believe computing is a super power for good, enabling access to information, economic opportunity, productivity, connectivity between friends and family and more. We think everyone in the world should have access to the best computing has to offer. We provide the platform for original equipment manufacturers (OEMs) and developers to build compelling computing devices (smartphones, tablets, TVs, wearables, etc) that run the best apps/services for everyone in the world.

Responsibilities

  • Identify and pursue new partnership opportunities to grow and generate business for the Android ecosystem, aligning with Google’s platform and devices strategic goals.
  • Develop, synthesize and share actionable local partner feedback and market/country trends to drive whole-Android and Platform and Devices (P&D) performance and partner with cross-functional teams to address and mitigate local challenges.
  • Drive operational excellence in all aspects of our partnership, regular business reviews, agreement integration, escalations management, etc. Serve as the main conduit for partner feedback and insights.
  • Lead complex, cross-functional business initiatives from conception to launch, ensuring they are delivered on time and to a high standard.
  • Guide the integration of internal team timelines, goals, and objectives. Partner with Product, Engineering, Legal, Marketing and other teams to execute on our overall P&D strategy.
